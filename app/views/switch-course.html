  1. Step 1: Who to speak to and when

    Course tutor: speak to your course tutor as soon as possible. They should be able to let you know how switching courses works at your college.

    Any teacher: If you're not getting on with your course tutor, it's okay to speak to any teacher you feel comfortable with.

  2. Step 2: What you might need to do

    The tutor or teacher will want to understand why you want to change course. It's a good idea to have a think about why you want to change and how long you've been considering it so you can talk about it.

    You might also need to put your request in writing.

  3. Step 3: How it works

    Your teacher or tutor will check if:

    • you can switch or if it's too late in the year
    • the course you want to do has space
    • switching would cause any clashes with your lessons

    They should help you create a plan, even if you cannot switch immediately — it's okay to be disappointed if that is the case. They should help you understand what your next step will be so you don't feel stuck.

    You might want to ask to speak to your college careers adviser. They can give you advice on:

    • changing course at your college or a different college
    • understanding different options
    • rethinking your goals

  4. Step 4: What you can say

    Here are a few prompts of what to say to a tutor or teacher about wanting to change course:

    "Can you explain to me how switching courses works?"

    "I want to change course. Are you the best person to speak to about that, or is there someone else I should speak to in college?"

    "I want to switch courses to [course name]. I think it will suit me better. Can you help me put a plan in place of how to get there?"

    "How do I make an appointment with a careers adviser?"
