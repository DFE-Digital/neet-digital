Find out who can help you as a young person

It can be exhausting having to keep resitting your maths GCSE. You’re not the only one. Over 200,000 young people in England sat their maths GCSE again in 2025.

Share or email page

Who to speak to and when

Maths teacher
Your maths teacher should be able to give you specific advice on what to focus on or improve.

Any teacher If you don’t feel you can speak to your course tutor, it’s okay to speak to any teacher or tutor you feel comfortable with. They should listen and help you create a plan.

Student support services Your college will likely have some form of pastoral officer or careers adviser you can speak to for help with your resit.

Review your last results with a teacher

You might find it useful to review your last exam paper with your maths teacher.

They can help you see what your strengths are, where things went wrong and specific topics you can improve.

Alternative to GCSE maths

If you’re struggling to pass after multiple tries, you might be able to do a functional skills qualification instead. This is equivalent to a grade 4 and is widely accepted as an alternative to a GCSE.

Your maths teacher or student support services can help see if you’re eligible.

You might want to ask to speak to a careers adviser. They can help you see if your next step after college accepts a functional skills qualification.

Understand your options for after college

Your maths teacher or student support services should help you understand what your options are if you do or do not pass maths.

They might refer you to a careers adviser so you can chat about your goals and help you create a plan.

There are options if you need to do your maths resit after college. You might be able to do a course in a library, adult education centre or other local venue.

You might want to speak to a careers adviser so you can chat about your goals. They can help you create a plan.

What you can ask

Here are a few prompts of what to say to get help with your maths resit:

“Are there any resources I can use that are different from what I’ve already tried?”

“Can I get any further help or tutoring to help me pass?”

“Can you help me work out what I need to focus on?”

“I saw you could do a foundation year at uni if you don’t pass maths. Can you help see if it’s an option for me?”

Talk to someone about your mental health

If you need help urgently, you do not have to deal with it by yourself.

There are people who can help you.
