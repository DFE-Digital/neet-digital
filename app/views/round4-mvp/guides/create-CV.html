I want to create a CV or make my CV better

If you’re struggling to know what to include in your CV, you’re not alone. Find out who can help you and see examples CVs from other young people.

Text or email this page

Who can help and when

Sometimes it can be confusing to know where to start. There’s no right or wrong answer of where to get advice from.

Careers adviser: You can chat to a National Careers Service careers adviser for free online or on the phone. They can look through your CV with you and give you advice on how to change your CV for the jobs you’re applying for.

Local support: There are often CV drop-in sessions in places like libraries, community centres and youth hubs. They might also tell you about services your local council provides. Your local councils might have a team of people who help young people to search for work, create CVs and practise for interviews.

Charities and organisations: Some organisations like Street League and King’s Trust have support workers who can help improve your CV. You can meet others in the same situation and join things like like workshops and free courses.

Ask people you know: If you can, see if a friend, family member or someone you know can read your CV and give you some feedback. It’s useful to get a fresh perspective. They might spot things you had not thought about, and give you some ideas of what to change.

Create a few tailored CVs for different types of jobs

We know tailoring your CV for every job application can feel exhausting.

If you have the energy to do it for every single one, that’s great. However, it’s common to lose motivation, especially if you’re been applying for a while.

We recommend you have a few tailored CVs for the types of jobs you’re applying for. You can make sure each CV emphasises the skills and experience needed for that type of role. This might help make it feel more manageable going forward.

For example, you might have a CV tailored towards:

  • working in a pub, restaurant or cafe
  • seasonal retail jobs in shops or superma
  • babysitting and nursery work experience

What you can ask

Here are a few prompts of what to say to a careers adviser or work coach about your CV:

“I have a CV but want to see if what I’m doing to tailor it works well or not”

“Do you have any example CVs you can show me of other people who don’t have lots of experience?”

“Can you help me understand what experience I can put on my CV?”
