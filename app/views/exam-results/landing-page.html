Get help if you’re finding college or sixth form hard

Things in college or sixth form do not always go to plan. See who can help you decide what to do next, change subjects or pass your GCSE maths or English resit.

How this tool can help you

55% of young people have said something would stop them asking for help.

Taking that step can be hard, especially if you're not sure who the right person to speak to is.

We can help you understand who to speak to.

Who would find this useful

This tool is for young people aged 16 to 24 who are having a hard time in college, sixth form, or with job applications.

You might find this useful if you’re:

  • not sure what to do when you leave college or sixth form
  • on a course you’re not enjoying or did not choose
  • stuck resitting GCSE maths or English over and over again

What to expect

You'll answer a few questions. Some questions are optional, such as your name. You can choose what you feel comfortable sharing.

It will take less than 5 minutes to complete.

At the end, you’ll get:

  • advice about what you can do
  • advice on who to speak to
  • suggestions of what to say or how to ask for help
