Cookies on the Find out who can help you as a young person prototype

Cookies are files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website.

We use cookies to make the Find out who can help you as a young person prototype work and collect information about how you use our service.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies keep your information secure while you use Find out who can help you as a young person. We do not need to ask permission to use them.

Name Purpose Expires
.AspNetCore_Session Used to store page data between HTTP requests at our server. When you close your browser
BNES_.AspNetCore_Session An encrypted copy of .AspNetCore_Session which is used by our firewall to provide an extra level of security. When you close your browser
cookies_policy Saves your cookie consent settings. 1 year
cookies_preferences_set Lets us know that you've saved your cookie consent settings. 1 year

Analytics cookies (optional)

Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to collect data about how you use this service. This information helps us to improve our service.

Google is not allowed to use or share our analytics data with anyone.

Google Analytics stores anonymised information about:

  • how you got to Find out who can help you as a young person
  • the pages you visit on Find out who can help you as a young person
  • how long you spend on each page
Name Purpose Expires
_ga Helps us count how many people visit this website by tracking whether you've visited before. 2 years
_gat Used to manage the rate at which page view requests are made. 1 minute
_gid Stores and updates a unique value for each page you've visited to help us improve your experience. 1 day

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how you use Find out who can help you as a young person. This information helps us to improve the prototype.

Microsoft Clarity uses the following cookies:

Name Purpose Expires
_clck Checks if you've visited the prototype before and makes sure you're given the same ID. 1 year
_clsk Connects a user's page views into a single session recording. 1 day
CLID Identifies the first time Microsoft Clarity saw this user on any website that uses Microsoft Clarity. 1 year
ANONCHK Indicates whether the MUID cookie is sent to ANID. ANID is not used by Microsoft Clarity and expires at the end of your session. End of session
MUID Identifies unique web browsers visiting Microsoft sites. These cookies are used for advertising, analytics and operational purposes. 1 year
MR Indicates whether to refresh the MUID cookie. 7 days
SM Used to synchronise the MUID cookie across Microsoft domains. End of session
