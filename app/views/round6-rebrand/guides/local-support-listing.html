  • BOOST

    1.2 Miles

    West Norfolk and Breckland

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • get help with finding a job, including your CV and mock interviews
    • take part in work experience opportunities
    • get 121 mentoring to help with confidence and work skills

    Who can get help

    You need to be:

    • age 16 to 24
    • not in education, employment or training

    How to contact

    Get in contact by:

    • using the online form for BOOST to self refer
    • emailing boost@norfolk.gov.uk for West Norfolk
    • emailing boostbreckland@norfolk.gov.uk for Breckland

  • Help You Choose

    2.4 Miles

    Norfolk

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • get help with making decisions about what you want to do next
    • see local career events, open days and jobs
    • get personalised help from an adviser if you’re not in education

    Who can get help

    You need to be a young person living in Norfolk.

    How to contact

    You can contact them by email, phone or online. View contact details for Help You Choose.

  • I can be a

    Norfolk and Suffolk (online)

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • discover job industries that are popular in the area
    • browse a directory of local employers
    • find local job opportunities that may not appear on larger job sites like Indeed

    Who can get help

    Young people in Norfolk and Suffolk up to the age of 25.

  • YES (Youth employment service)

    3.5 Miles

    East Suffolk

    Support you can get

    You can get a coach to help you with your next step. This can be anything from figuring out your next step to feeling more confident with job applications.

    Who can get help

    You need to be aged 16 to 24 and live in East Suffolk to get a coach.

    How to contact

    You can book a session with a coach. You can choose a date and time that works for you.

  • The Source

    1.2 Miles

    Suffolk

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • get help if you feel stuck or are unsure what to do next
    • get help finding a job, including details of local job fairs
    • find out about other local services
    • access personalised adviser support by phone or email

    Who can get help

    You need to:

    • be aged 11 to 19, or up to 25 if you have additional needs
    • live in Suffolk

    How to contact

    Get in contact by using the online form for The Source.

  • Volunteer Suffolk

    East Suffolk

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • search for volunteering opportunities across Suffolk
    • join the youth volunteering programme if you’re aged 14 to 25

    How to contact

    You can call 01473 345 400 or email volunteering@communityactionsuffolk.org.uk to join the youth programme.

  • Work Hub on GOV.UK

    Support you can get

    You can:

    • use an AI work assistant to get feedback on a job application
    • build a CV
    • speak to a careers adviser who can help you work out your next step

    Who can get help

    You can speak to a careers adviser if you’re aged 18 or older or not in education or training. You can see who you can speak to depending on your situation.

    How to contact

    You can speak to a DWP careers adviser on 0800 072 088.
